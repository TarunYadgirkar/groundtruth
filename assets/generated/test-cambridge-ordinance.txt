CITY OF CAMBRIDGE, MASSACHUSETTS
ORDINANCE NO. 2026-T1 (FICTIONAL)

AN ORDINANCE ADDING CHAPTER 8.80 TO THE CAMBRIDGE MUNICIPAL CODE, "PROHIBITION ON ALGORITHMIC RENT-SETTING"

Ordained by the City Council of the City of Cambridge, Massachusetts, on September 29, 2026.

Section 8.80.010 Definitions.
"Algorithmic device" means any software, system, or process that uses nonpublic competitor data to recommend or set rent amounts, lease terms, or occupancy levels for residential dwelling units.

Section 8.80.020 Prohibition.
No owner or manager of a residential rental property containing five or more dwelling units in the City of Cambridge shall use an algorithmic device to set or recommend the rent for any dwelling unit.

Section 8.80.030 Exemptions.
This chapter shall not apply to dwelling units in buildings for which a certificate of occupancy was first issued on or after January 1, 2016.

Section 8.80.040 Enforcement.
A tenant or the City Solicitor may bring a civil action for violation of this chapter. Each violation is subject to a civil penalty of up to $1,000 per unit per month.

Section 8.80.050 Effective date.
This chapter shall take effect on March 1, 2027.
